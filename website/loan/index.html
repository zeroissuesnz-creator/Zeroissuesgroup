Standard method uses annual rate ÷ payments per year, constant rates and end-of-period repayments. First date labels the first regular payment; irregular initial periods, fees and balloon payments are excluded. Rule of 78 allocates the contract's total finance charge by descending weights. Enter the contractual payment if known. If left blank, a payment is estimated from the annual rate and term using the standard repayment formula, then its finance charge is allocated by Rule of 78. When a payment is entered, that payment determines total interest; the annual rate does not change the contractual total.
