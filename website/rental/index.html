Profit and cash flow are different

Profit = rent and reimbursements − operating expenses − chattels depreciation. Cash flow = income − cash operating expenses − mortgage principal − capital purchases. This assumes entered income and cash expenses were received/paid; it does not reconcile receivables, deposits or bank opening/closing balances.

Use an accountant-reviewed depreciation amount for eligible chattels. Land and building depreciation is excluded. A loss here does not automatically reduce tax on wages; residential rental excess deduction rules may apply.
